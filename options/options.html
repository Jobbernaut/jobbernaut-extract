Jobbernaut Extract Settings

Customize your job data export format

Available Fields

Use these placeholders in your template. They will be replaced with actual job data when you extract:

{job_id} Auto-generated unique ID
{job_title} Job title from posting
{company_name} Company name
{location} Job location
{status} Always "pending"
{posting_link} URL of job posting
{job_description} Full job description (formatted with pipe syntax)
